Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

Wish Fountain ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Wish Fountain mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use Wish Fountain:

  • Account information: Email address, username, and password (encrypted)
  • Profile information: Your chosen avatar, dreamer type, and onboarding preferences
  • Content you create: Wishes you post, messages in bottles you send, and reactions
  • Usage data: App interactions, feature usage, and session information
  • Device information: Device type, operating system, and app version

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the Wish Fountain service
  • To deliver messages in bottles between users
  • To send account-related emails (confirmation, password reset)
  • To personalize your fountain experience based on your preferences
  • To ensure the safety and security of our community

3. Anonymous Features

Wish Fountain is designed with anonymity in mind. When you post a wish anonymously or send a bottle to a stranger, your identity is not revealed to other users. We maintain anonymity as a core feature of our service.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a trusted database provider with industry-standard security measures. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share data with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the app (Supabase, Resend for emails)
  • Law enforcement when required by law

6. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Wishes expire after their set duration. You may request deletion of your account and data at any time by contacting us.

7. Children's Privacy

Wish Fountain is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Your Rights

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt out of non-essential communications
