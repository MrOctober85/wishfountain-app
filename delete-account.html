Delete Your Account

You can delete your account directly from within the app. Follow the steps below.

1
Open the Wish Fountain app on your device
2
Tap the Profile icon at the bottom of the screen
3
Scroll down and tap Settings
4
Tap Delete Account at the bottom
5
Confirm your decision — your account will be permanently deleted
⚠️ Warning: Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. All your wishes, bottles, stars, coins, and profile data will be permanently removed within 30 days. Purchase records may be kept by Google Play and RevenueCat as required by law.
