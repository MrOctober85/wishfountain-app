Child Safety Standards

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Our Commitment

Wish Fountain is committed to providing a safe environment for all users and maintaining zero tolerance for any content that exploits or harms children. We comply with all applicable child safety laws and regulations.

Age Requirements

Wish Fountain is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly allow children under the age of 13 to create accounts or use our service.

Users who are found to be under the minimum age requirement will have their accounts suspended immediately.

Prohibited Content

The following content is strictly prohibited on Wish Fountain:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind
  • Content that exploits, sexualizes, or endangers minors
  • Content that facilitates grooming or inappropriate contact with minors
  • Any content that violates child safety laws

Reporting Mechanisms

Wish Fountain provides in-app reporting tools that allow users to report any content or behavior that may violate our child safety standards:

  • Report buttons on all user-generated content (wishes and bottles)
  • Direct reporting via email: support@wishfountain.app
  • All reports are reviewed promptly by our team

Enforcement Actions

When violations are detected, we take immediate action including:

  • Immediate removal of violating content
  • Permanent suspension of offending accounts
  • Reporting to relevant national and regional authorities
  • Cooperation with law enforcement investigations

Reporting to Authorities

Wish Fountain reports child sexual abuse material (CSAM) to the appropriate national and regional authorities, including the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant local law enforcement agencies.

Contact

For child safety concerns, please contact our designated safety officer:

  • Email: support@wishfountain.app
  • Response time: Within 24 hours

For emergency situations involving immediate danger to a child, please contact your local law enforcement immediately.
